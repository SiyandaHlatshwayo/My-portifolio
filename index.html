About Me - Siyanda Hlatshwayo

About Me

My name is Siyanda Hlatshwayo, and I hold an Honours degree in Hydrology and a Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Geography and Hydrology. I specialise in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), remote sensing, water resource management, and environmental data analysis, using tools such as ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Google Earth Engine, Google Earth, and Python to analyse and solve environmental challenges. I also have experience in database management, including SQL, database automation, and connecting spatial databases with GIS platforms to improve data management and analysis. In addition, I have skills in web development, where I create clean, responsive, and modern websites and interactive applications. My goal is to combine my hydrology, GIS, and technical skills to develop sustainable, innovative, and data-driven solutions for water and environmental management.
